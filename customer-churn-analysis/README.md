📉 Customer Churn Analysis | Power BI Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Customer Churn Analysis dashboard built in Power BI. The goal is to identify patterns, behaviors, and key drivers behind customer churn in a telecom/business service environment.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to understand why customers leave, which segments are at risk, and how business decisions can reduce churn.

🎯 Objectives
* Measure overall churn rate and customer retention
* Identify key reasons for customer churn
* Analyze churn across demographics, geography, and customer segments
* Evaluate the impact of pricing, contracts, and services on churn
* Provide insights to improve customer retention strategies

📈 Dashboard Insights
🔍 Churn Drivers
* The main reason for churn is competition (better offers, devices, or pricing)
* Customer service issues (support attitude, expertise) also significantly impact churn
* Pricing and product dissatisfaction contribute to customer loss

👥 Customer Segments
* Senior customers have the highest churn rate (~38%)
* Younger customers show relatively lower churn
* Certain age groups exhibit extremely high churn spikes (>50%)

📄 Contract Impact
* Monthly contracts: ~46% churn rate
* Yearly contracts: ~6.6% churn rate

➡️ Long-term contracts significantly improve retention

🌍 Geographic Trends
* Churn varies by state, with some regions exceeding 30% churn rate
* Geographic segmentation helps identify high-risk markets

📊 Service & Behavior Insights
* Customers with unlimited data plans show higher churn (~32%)
* Frequent customer service calls correlate with higher churn
* Overtime charges and extra fees negatively impact retention

📊 Dashboard Features
* Interactive filters (state, contract type, demographics)
* KPI cards for quick performance overview
* Churn breakdown by reason and category
* Geographic map visualization
* Drill-down analysis by customer segment
* Multi-page analytical structure

💡 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to analyze customer churn and uncover actionable insights.

It highlights that churn is driven not only by pricing but also by customer experience, contract structure, and competitive pressure, providing a strong foundation for retention strategies.
