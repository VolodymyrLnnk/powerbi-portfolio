Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze key performance metrics of an e-commerce business. The goal is to uncover insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and regional distribution.

The dashboard enables users to explore data dynamically, identify growth opportunities, and support data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Objectives
* Analyze overall sales performance and revenue trends
* Identify top-performing products and categories
* Evaluate customer purchasing behavior
* Explore geographical distribution of sales
* Detect seasonality and sales patterns

📈 Key Insights
* Certain product categories consistently generate the highest revenue
* Sales show clear seasonal trends with peak periods
* A small group of customers contributes a significant share of total revenue
* Regional analysis highlights top-performing markets and underperforming areas
* Discounts and promotions strongly influence purchasing behavior

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* Power BI
* Power Query (data cleaning and transformation)
* DAX (data modeling and calculations)

📊 Dashboard Features
* Interactive filters (date, region, category)
* KPI cards for quick performance overview
* Sales trend analysis over time
* Top products and categories visualization
* Geographic sales distribution

💡 Conclusion

This dashboard demonstrates how Power BI can be used to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful insights. It highlights key business drivers and supports strategic decision-making through intuitive visualizations.
