👥 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive HR Analytics dashboard built in Power BI to analyze workforce data, employee performance, and attrition trends.

The dashboard provides HR teams and business leaders with actionable insights into employee demographics, satisfaction levels, and retention risks, enabling data-driven workforce management.

🎯 Objectives
* Monitor key HR metrics (headcount, attrition, active employees)
* Analyze employee demographics (age, gender, marital status, ethnicity)
* Track hiring trends over time
* Evaluate employee performance and satisfaction
* Identify key drivers of employee attrition

📊 Dashboard Structure
1. Overview
* Total Employees: 1470
* Active Employees: 1233
* Attrition Rate: 16.1%
* Hiring trends across years
* Employee distribution by department

2. Demographics
* Age distribution (from <20 to 50+)
* Gender breakdown (including non-binary and undisclosed)
* Marital status analysis
* Ethnicity distribution with average salary comparison

3. Performance Tracker
* Individual employee performance tracking
* Metrics include:
   * Job Satisfaction
   * Work-Life Balance
   * Manager Rating
   * Environment Satisfaction
* Ratings tracked over multiple years

4. Attrition Analysis
* Attrition rate by department and job role
* Impact of:
   * Overtime
   * Business travel frequency
   * Tenure (years at company)
* Attrition trends over time

📈 Key Insights
* Attrition rate (16.1%) indicates potential retention challenges
* Higher attrition observed in specific departments and job roles
* Employees with frequent travel and overtime show increased attrition risk
* Attrition is higher among employees with shorter tenure
* Workforce is primarily concentrated in Technology and Sales departments
* Salary differences exist across ethnic groups, highlighting diversity considerations

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* Power BI
* Power Query (data transformation)
* DAX (calculated measures and KPIs)

📊 Dashboard Features
* Interactive filters and slicers
* KPI cards for quick insights
* Time-based trend analysis
* Drill-down capabilities by department and employee
* Multi-page analytical structure
