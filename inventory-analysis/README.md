📦 Inventory Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing inventory performance, product profitability, and stock efficiency using Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into revenue, cost of goods sold (COGS), inventory turnover, and product segmentation using ABC classification.

The goal is to help businesses optimize inventory management, identify high-value products, and improve operational efficiency.

🎯 Objectives
* Analyze inventory performance and sales trends
* Identify high-value and low-value products
* Evaluate inventory turnover efficiency
* Support data-driven decisions for stock optimization
* Reduce overstocking and understocking risks

📌 Key Features & Analysis

🔹 1. Revenue vs COGS Analysis
* Compared Revenue and Total COGS across products
* Identified products with high margins vs low profitability
* Highlighted cost-heavy items impacting overall performance

🔹 2. ABC Classification
* Products segmented into:
  * A (High Value) – top revenue contributors
  * B (Medium Value)
  * C (Low Value)
* Majority of revenue is generated by a small group of A-class items

🔹 3. Inventory Turnover Analysis
* Measured how efficiently inventory is sold and replaced
* Identified:
  * Fast-moving products (high turnover)
  * Slow-moving or stagnant inventory (low turnover)
 
🔹 4. Category Performance
* Compared:
  * Revenue by category
  * Inventory turnover by category

🔹 5. Product-Level Insights
* Identified top-performing SKUs
* Highlighted products with:
  * High sales but low margins
  * Low demand and poor turnover

📈 Key Insights
* A-class products drive the majority of revenue, confirming the Pareto principle
* Some products have high COGS-to-revenue ratios, reducing profitability
* Inventory turnover varies significantly, indicating inefficiencies in stock management
* Certain categories outperform others in both revenue and efficiency
* Opportunities exist to:
  * Optimize stock levels
  * Remove or discount slow-moving items
  * Focus on high-margin products
