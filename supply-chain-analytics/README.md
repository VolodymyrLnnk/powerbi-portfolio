# Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

## 🔎 Quick Summary
- Domain: Supply Chain
- Goal: Optimize supplier selection and production strategy
- Tools: Power BI, DAX, Power Query

---

## 📊 Business Problem
The company needs to decide:
- Which suppliers to choose
- Whether to make or buy products
- How to optimize costs

---

## 📈 Solution
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard with:
- KPI tracking
- Scenario analysis
- Supplier comparison
